# Refund Policy — UMBRA PRO

_Last updated: 2026-11-07. This policy supplements the EULA (`LICENSE`); where they conflict, the EULA controls._

## The promise

If UMBRA PRO does not work for you, tell us within **14 days of purchase** and we will refund the full purchase price — no interrogation, no "uninstall first" hoops beyond confirming your license key so we can deactivate it.

## How to request a refund

1. Contact the publisher's support channel you purchased from (see the storefront's GET PRO section).
2. Include your license key (`jarvis license status` shows it is active; the key itself identifies the purchase).
3. Optionally attach a `jarvis diagnostics bundle` — it helps us improve, and it contains no keys and no message bodies.
4. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, typically within 5 business days.

## What is not refundable

- Requests more than 14 days after purchase (evaluated case-by-case for legal compliance — consumer rights in your jurisdiction may extend this).
- Licenses revoked for EULA violations (fraud, redistribution, circumventing the license check).

## Local data

Your data is yours: it never leaves your machine as part of a refund, and nothing is wiped remotely. After a refund your key stops validating and UMBRA returns to LITE — your memories, health data and conversations remain on your device.
